# Demonstration Guide (10–15 minutes)

1. Open **Executive Dashboard** and establish the KPI hierarchy.
2. Explain purchase ROC-AUC, top-decile lift, and aggregate demand/revenue/GP error without calling ROC-AUC accuracy.
3. Open **Recommendation Explorer** and select a real `PricingDecisionID`.
4. Walk through Current → Model Optimal → Business Rules → Promotion/Markdown/Inventory → Final Price.
5. Open **Price Scenario Simulator** and show the expected gross-profit curve and support envelope.
6. In **AI Pricing Agent**, ask: “Why is this the recommended price?”
7. Ask: “What happens if I use another supported price?”
8. Ask: “How well is the model performing?”
9. Open **Decision Audit** and show manual-review and action filters.
10. Close with the business value: governed, explainable, advisory pricing intelligence with no automatic writeback.

Suggested questions:

- What does this solution do?
- How strong is our purchase model?
- What price should we offer for this decision?
- Why is the recommendation higher than the current price?
- How do business rules affect the model-optimal price?
- What happens if I reduce the price by 5%?
- Show seasonal slow-moving markdown recommendations.
- Which decisions require manual review?
- What is the expected gross profit at the recommended price?
